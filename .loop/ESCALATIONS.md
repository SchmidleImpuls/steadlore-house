# Escalations

Escalate rather than force consent when a change is:

- irreversible or hard to roll back
- legal, licensing, privacy, or compliance relevant
- security-sensitive or secret-handling relevant
- brand, identity, naming, or positioning defining
- likely to affect lockout risk or core infrastructure safety
- a product strategy change rather than an increment
- a process change without recurring evidence

Escalation record format:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>

- Run:
- Trigger:
- Decision needed:
- Options:
- Recommendation:
- Decider:
- Outcome:
```
